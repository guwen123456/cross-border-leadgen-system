# 开发信与询价模板（全部人工审核后逐封发送，不群发）

占位符：{Company} {Name} {Product} {Model} {Detail} —— {Detail} 必须是针对这家公司查到的一个具体事实，填不出就先别发。

## 1. 给海外买家：首次开发信（EN）

Subject: {Model} TWS earbuds — small MOQ trial from Shenzhen

Hi {Name},

I noticed {Company} sells {Detail}. We work with Shenzhen factories on the {Model} (Bluetooth 5.3, {battery} playtime, CE/FCC documents available for this model).

What we can offer: trial orders from 100 pcs, mixed colors, and direct shipping to your warehouse. I can send the full spec sheet, tiered pricing and a sample quote if useful.

Worth a look?

Best regards,
Richard — Shenzhen, China

## 2. 给海外买家：跟进信（3–4 天无回复时，EN）

Subject: Re: {Model} TWS earbuds — small MOQ trial from Shenzhen

Hi {Name}, just floating this back up. I can send pricing for 100 / 500 / 1,000 pcs plus a sample option — no commitment needed. If earbuds aren't your category right now, a quick "not now" is fine too.

Richard

## 3. 给海外买家：报价信骨架（EN，数字必须先过 orders_profit.csv）

Subject: Quotation — {Model} × {Qty} pcs

Hi {Name}, here is the full landed breakdown for {Qty} pcs of {Model}: unit price, shipping to {Destination}, lead time {Days} days after payment confirmation, and what after-sales covers. Total: {Total}. If the total works, next step is a sample or a small trial order.

## 4. 给国内工厂：询价（中文，微信/邮件通用）

您好，我在深圳做外贸客户开发，正在给一款真无线蓝牙耳机找长期供货。想确认 6 件事：1）主力型号和参数（蓝牙版本、续航、防水）；2）100/500/1000 台的阶梯价和报价有效期；3）MOQ，是否支持小批量和单件代发；4）现货还是排产，交期多久；5）CE/FCC 认证文件能否提供、对应哪个型号；6）质量问题和退货的售后责任怎么划分。资料发我后我会认真核对，有合适的海外客户会优先推贵司的产品。

试点包 299 元：30 家已核验买家 + 10 封个性化开发信 + 1 封跟进信模板。可以先免费给您看 5 家样例，您觉得质量行再决定。先说明：我保证的是核验质量和开发信可直接用，不承诺一定回复或成交——谁承诺这个谁就是忽悠。
